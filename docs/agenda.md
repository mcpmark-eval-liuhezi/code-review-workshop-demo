# Workshop Agenda: GitHub Review Workflow

1. **Welcome & intros** — who we are, what you'll walk away with (5 min)
2. **Review basics** — why we review code, what a good review looks like (10 min)
3. **Tour of the repository** — branches, pull requests, and the Files changed tab (10 min)
4. **Live demo: opening a pull request** — from branch to PR, watching CI go green (15 min)
5. **Live demo: drafting a pending review** — anchoring line comments to the diff (15 min)
6. **Live demo: submitting the review** — comment vs. approve vs. request changes (10 min)
7. **Hands-on: pair up and review each other's PRs** (20 min)
8. **Q&A** — bring your own repo questions (10 min)
9. **Wrap-up** — recap, cheat sheet handout, and what to practice next week (5 min)
